# **Problem Context**

At Roger Healthcare, we work with Medicare-certified home health agencies that care for patients in their own homes. They send nurses out to visit patients in their homes a few times each week, for the duration of 60-day episodes. Sometimes patients can have multiple episodes in a row.

To get paid for this care, the agency sends **claims** to the patient’s insurance payer (for example, Medicare).

<u>Helpful starting resources (optional and dense but can help your AI agent to research) both files in docs/ dir:</u>

- CMS Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 10: Home Health Agency Billing (how home health claims work end to end) 
- CMS Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 25: Completing the UB-04 (CMS-1450) Data Set (what a claim contains, field by field; the 837I is the electronic version of the same claim)

# **Problem**

Make a functional prototype using your preferred prototyping tool (AI strongly encouraged!) of a very simple ‘Billing Manager’ UI that a billing team can use to track system-generated home healthcare claims, make sure they are complete and ready to send, send them out to payers, and track their success or failure. You can assume that you are working in an EHR system that already has data records for patients and their completed episodes (and thus some of those episodes can have billable claims sent out).

This may involve some industry research about home healthcare billing and claims, and you are free to draw on inspiration from existing EHRs.

We recognize this is a very ambitious ask for 2 hours and that you will have to make lots of compromises to get something ready. You do not need to include all functionality needed, but make sure to include the most important flows or entities and model them well.

**The details matter!** Rather than a vague and ‘handwavey’ UX, we care that you took time to understand home health billing well and that your prototype tries its best to fulfill the core flows needed for a day-to-day billing team. Do this as best as you can using the research avenues available to you. We will discuss your design and thought process further during the next interview.

## **Design Guidance**

Users in this industry value simplicity, clear & transparent UI, and less text. They are not tech savvy and some may not have perfect English proficiency.
